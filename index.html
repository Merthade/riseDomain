How It Works

Your morning in four steps

Rise turns your morning into a ritual. Each step flows naturally into the next.

1

Alarm

Set your wake time. Rise uses system-level alarms that work even in silent mode.

2

Wake-Up Check

A gentle 10-minute check to make sure you're actually up. Miss it and the alarm returns.

3

Focus Timer

Settle into a focused session, 5 to 120 minutes. Read, journal, meditate, or just breathe.

4

Done

Complete your routine and build your streak. Reflect on how the morning went.

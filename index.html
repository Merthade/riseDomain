Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Designed to be calm, focused, and distraction-free.

Smart Alarms

Real system-level alarms powered by AlarmKit. They ring on time, even in silent mode.

Focus Sessions

Short sessions free, up to 120 minutes with Pro. No rigid Pomodoro rules. Your pace, your way.

Wind Down

A calm evening session with ambient sound to ease you out of the day. Free to try, longer with Pro.

Streak Tracking

Build momentum every morning. Calendar history, milestone titles, and streak freezes for the days life gets in the way.

40+ Identity Titles

Earn titles for your routine. Early Bird, Marathoner, Routinist, and more. One worn at a time in your morning greeting.

An optional thought at the end of each session. Quietly stacks up so you can look back weeks later.

Records your wake times and focus sessions to Health. Your morning data, alongside everything else.

iCloud Backup

Your settings, streak, and journal back up privately to your iCloud. Restore on a new device in one tap.

Widgets & Live Activities
